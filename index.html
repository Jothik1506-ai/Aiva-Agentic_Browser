Welcome to Aiva

The agentic browser from AIVA Freelancia — it doesn't just show you the web, it can act on it. A quick tour of what's here before you dive in.

🤖

Aiva Agentic Assistant

Open the chat panel (bottom-right) and ask Aiva to do something, not just answer a question — it can navigate, read the page, click buttons, fill and submit forms, and manage tabs on your behalf. Every step is narrated, and you can stop it anytime.

⌘

Command Palette

Press Ctrl K anywhere to fuzzy-search your open tabs, shortcuts, and browser commands — or hand a query straight to Aiva without leaving the keyboard.

◧

Sidebar & Spaces

Pin tabs, drag to reorder, and switch between color-coded Personal / Work / Research spaces. Hover the left edge to reveal it, or pin it open from the icon in the top bar.

🌿

Wellness, built in

Face Scanner (fatigue detection), Focus Mode, and Health & Care live in Settings (•••) in the sidebar — all opt-in, all off until you turn them on.
